Origins of Nail Art

  • Ancient Egypt:

    Royalty used deep red dyes to signify status. Queen Nefertiti and Cleopatra were known for bold nail colors.

  • Babylon:

    Warriors stained their nails before battle to show strength and class.

  • China:

    Nail colors indicated social status. Royals used gold, silver, and elaborate decorations.

Evolution of Nail Art

  • 1920s:

    The first modern nail polish was introduced, inspired by car paint. The “moon manicure” became trendy.

  • 1950s:

    Bold colors and home nail kits became popular in households.

  • 1970s-80s:

    Artificial nails, acrylics, and DIY kits surged in popularity.

  • 1990s:

    Hip hop culture introduced vibrant custom designs with rhinestones and decals.

  • 2000s–2010s:

    Social media sparked a global nail art trend with Instagram, YouTube, and Pinterest.
